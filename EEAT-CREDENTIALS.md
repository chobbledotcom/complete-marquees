## 4. Expertise (specialist knowledge demonstrated)

- **Capri & Pagoda marquee specialists** — modern alternatives to traditional
  marquees, with the technical detail to back it up.
- **Concrete product knowledge** from the FAQ page:
  - All marquees come with **side walls as standard: half clear, half solid**,
    configurable on the day (on/off/half-and-half).
  - **Capri arches are approx. 7–8ft high; centre pole approx. 16ft.**
  - **Waterproof** (with the honest caveat: not in stormy weather).
  - **Cannot be attached to buildings** due to their shape — honest limitation.
  - **Must be sited on flat grass** with no trees/bushes/hedges through the footprint.
  - **No flooring needed in summer** depending on grass condition; flooring required
    under a dance floor to protect it.
  - **Decorating guidance**: use string, not sticky tape/staples; no crepe paper
    (it stains the marquee).
- **Linking capability** — Capri marquees can be **linked together** to cover large
  or irregular areas and form custom shapes/sizes (stated on nearly every service
  page). This is a recurring expertise/flexibility point.
- **Four core Capri sizes plus Pagoda**, with the option to join multiples for
  bigger footprints (FAQ / Sizes & Prices).
- **Consultative, bespoke approach** — "we work closely with each client",
  "we design marquees to match your vision, space, and guest numbers" (Our Marquees).
