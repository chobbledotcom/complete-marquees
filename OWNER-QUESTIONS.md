## 1. Insurance and paperwork

1. **(essential)** Who's the £10 million public liability with, and have you got a copy of the certificate we can show customers on request? A scan or photo of the certificate is fine. We'd put "insured to £10 million, certificate available on request" on every page.
2. Renewal date on the PL policy, roughly. Helps us make sure copy doesn't go stale.
3. Does the marquee fabric carry a fire-retardancy certificate from the supplier? BS 7837 or DIN 4102 B1 are the usual ones. If you've got a paper or PDF from whoever sold you the marquees, that's what we're after.
4. Anything else you've signed up to that we could mention. SafeContractor? CHAS? Construction Line? Any council approved-supplier lists?

## 2. The two of you

5. **(essential)** A photo of each of you. Phone snaps are fine. Standing next to a marquee, holding a mug of tea, doesn't matter, just something honest.
6. Three or four sentences each on how you got into this and what you do day to day. David, are you mainly workshop and install? Joanne, mainly bookings and customers? Or does it overlap? We're trying to put a face to the business rather than another anonymous "team page".
7. Anyone else permanent on the install team you'd want named on the site?

## 3. How you actually work

8. Do you offer to come and look at the garden before someone books? Is that free, by appointment, weekends, weekdays? A planner reading the site wants to know.
9. Typical lead time on a booking, roughly. Weeks? Months? Different for weddings vs other events?
10. Deposit, cancellation, weather. Do you have a one-page set of standard terms we could publish? If not, a couple of bullet points on each from you and we'll draft something for you to approve.
11. Payment methods you take. Bank transfer? Card? Cheque?

## 4. The £330 sidebar

12. **(essential)** The right-hand sidebar on every page currently says *"Packages from only £330"*. The cheapest package on the prices page is £390 plus VAT. Is the £330 out of date, or is it a different basis (marquee-only, smallest possible hire, etc.)? Quick word and we'll line them up.
