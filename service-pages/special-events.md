      ### Marquees and what they come with

      Our Capri marquees come in four sizes, from a 20 by 20 foot setup that seats around 20 people up to a 28 by 58 foot marquee that takes 120 seated guests. We can link several together to cover a bigger or awkward space, and every marquee has half-clear, half-solid side walls as standard, so you can open them up on a warm day or keep the weather out if it turns.

      We can add flooring, tables, chairs, a dance floor, staging, lighting and heaters, and we deliver, install and collect everything ourselves. The marquee has to go on flat grass with nothing growing through the footprint, and we don't put it up in stormy weather or attach it to a building. Our packages start from £390 for a 20 by 20 foot setup, and you'll find the full list on our [packages and prices](/packages/) page, with the individual sizes on the [sizes and prices](/sizes-prices/) page.

      If you're arranging a [wedding](/wedding-marquees/), a [garden party](/party-marquees/) or a [corporate event](/corporate-event-marquees/), the same setup applies. Lots of our special events have entertainment alongside the marquee, and our sister company [Monster Event Hire](https://www.monstereventhire.co.uk) supplies bouncy castles, photo booths, rodeo bulls and assault courses. Same family, same team, so you can sort the whole day through one call.

      To check availability and get a quote for your event in Hampshire, Surrey or West Sussex, call us on **01428 751 745** or [get in touch](/contact-us/). We'll confirm your date and talk through the setup.
