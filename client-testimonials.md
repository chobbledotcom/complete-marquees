      These are reviews from real customers, left on Google after we've delivered and collected a marquee or set up their entertainment. We're Joanne and David Morris, a family business based in Havant, and we've been hiring out Capri and Pagoda marquees for weddings, parties and corporate events across Hampshire, Surrey and West Sussex since 2002.

      The reviews cover both sides of the family business. Our sister company, [Monster Event Hire](https://www.monstereventhire.co.uk), looks after the bouncy castles, photo booths and other entertainment for the same events, so a review that mentions those is one of ours too.

      If you're planning a [wedding](/wedding-marquees/), a [party](/party-marquees/) or a [corporate event](/corporate-event-marquees/), read what people say below, then look at our [packages and prices](/packages/) or [get in touch](/contact-us/) with any questions. We cover the whole of [Hampshire](/areas/hampshire/) and the surrounding counties.

      The marquees behind the reviews are Capris in four sizes, from a 20 by 20 foot setup that seats 20 up to a 28 by 58 foot run that seats 120, plus a 6m Pagoda, all with half-clear, half-solid side walls. A 20 by 20 takes a garden party of 20 to 25, the 28 by 38 seats 80, and linked runs cover the 150-guest weddings; the [sizes and prices](/sizes-prices/) page has every number. They all need flat grass with no trees, bushes or hedges through the footprint, and we don't put anything up in stormy weather. Judith's review sums up what people come back for: "your team were polite on set up and take down, if we ever need a marquee again, we know where to call".
